Daanvia 0.5 · phone app redesign

Three directions for the new Daanvia phone app

Each direction is a full rebuild of every phone screen: explore, search, route preview, riding and bike setup. The engine underneath stays exactly as it is today. The screens below are live mockups: routes draw in, the riding view follows the bike with a tilted camera, and sheets and lists animate the way they would on the phone.

A

Gabi · night glass

Dark map by default, so the phone does not glare on the handlebar at night. Controls float on frosted glass over a full-bleed map. The route is a glowing amber line, taken from road-sign yellow. It is calm, premium and cinematic.

B

Daylight · soft sheets

Bright and friendly, in the style of current Apple and Google Maps, and readable in Cebu noon sun. It keeps Daanvia’s green as the brand and route colour. Big rounded sheets carry the content, and the greeting is in Cebuano: “Asa ta?” means “Where are we going?”

Hanken GroteskLight-first, with a dark mode at nightBottom sheets that hand off to each otherFlat heading-up cameraBig tap targets
C

Cockpit · glove-ready console

Built like an instrument cluster. The map sits on top and a heavy black console holds the controls at thumb height, with tiles at least 64 dp tall for riding gloves. Starting and ending a ride use press-and-hold, so a bump in a phone mount can't trigger them. Safety orange, monospace readouts, big numerals.

Figtree Black + JetBrains MonoLight map with a black consolePress-and-hold to start and endNorth-up or heading-upGauge-style readouts

All three use the same structure: one map screen with sheets on top instead of separate pages and pop-up menus. Scanning the dashboard QR, testing the connection and the transport logs move under Bike › Support, so the main flow is just choose a place, check the route, ride. Place names and road names come from the bundled Metro Cebu data. The ₱20/hr rate is an example of an owner-saved rate. Spots without a saved price show no price, as they do today.
