 DAANVIA
 Built-in navigation for your motorcycle display

 Revision 3: offline navigation, parking pins and adaptive TFT layouts
 Updated 19 October 2026 | Cebu first, Philippines

 Decision
 Move from the proven connection prototype to a navigation MVP. Preserve the working Wi-Fi Direct /
 H.264 transport; keep the scanner and logs under setup and support. The baseline is open-source and
 on-device. The owner prefers more engineering over recurring per-trip/provider fees. Commercial
 navigation SDKs are alternatives only, not a prerequisite.

 What is proven
 Owner tests reported visible output from an independent app on one ATR 160 dashboard and one
 OPPO Android 16 phone. The latest reported pattern and mirror sessions ran to their 45-second and
 90-second test limits. This establishes transport feasibility for that combination; it does not establish
 long-ride reliability or support for every firmware revision.

 What we are building
 Connect bike > choose destination > compare routes > start ride. The phone owns GPS, routing,
 voice and rendering. A dedicated navigation scene goes to the bike. Near the destination, arrival
 mode passively shows parking pins with motorcycle prices directly on the map before arrival. No
 parking footer, details panel or selection controls; missing prices have no price label. Final navigation
 must work without screen-capturing another app.

 Working identity
 Daanvia combines the Filipino word for road with a route-oriented name. Three original visual
 directions and logo concepts accompany this brief. The name is provisional: a web search is not
 trademark clearance, domain ownership or app-store reservation. Do IPOPHL and relevant
 international trademark searches before spending on launch branding. Generated artwork remains a
 concept until refined and checked for distinctiveness.

 Recommendation
 Adopt OpenStreetMap-derived regional data and MapLibre Native. Evaluate Valhalla as the first
 on-device routing candidate, with GraphHopper as a comparison; choose through an Android
 integration spike, not a server-only demo. Include offline search and parking prices near the
 destination in the MVP. Internet services and AI are optional enhancements, never required for a basic
 ride.

 Reading this brief
 Published facts carry source IDs. Architecture, budgets, quality thresholds and rollout steps are
 recommendations. Prices exclude tax and currency conversion. None of the vendor contacts, group
 posts, purchases or paid subscriptions described here has been sent or created.

 A region package is more than map tiles
 Package map graphics, routing graph, POI/search indexes and parking/rate data with compatible
 versions and separate layers. Map tiles alone cannot reroute. MBTiles/PMTiles may store map
 graphics, subject to the selected Android reader; they are not the routing graph. Build and test an
 actual supported local file reader. [S17,S26,S27]

 Commercial providers remain optional
 Google/TomTom/HERE/Mapbox are comparison or optional licensed enhancement paths. Do not
 combine their route/POI/traffic content with an OSM map without checking the exact agreement. No
 provider fee is required for the proposed offline baseline. Public OSM tile servers prohibit offline bulk
 prefetch; use lawful packages or our own generation/hosting. [S6-S16,S32,S33]

 A calm, usable navigation experience
 Primary flow: one-time QR setup, destination search, route preview, Start ride, automatic dashboard
 connection/recovery, and an obvious End ride action. No mandatory account for local-only navigation
 unless a selected provider or commercial feature requires one.

 Phone while parked
 Use Material 3 conventions, familiar back behavior and one dominant action per screen. Show
 destination entrance, travel time, distance and selected motorcycle profile before starting. Choose
 planned parking duration, walking limit and price preference before departure; show a route preview
 and expected parking costs. Arrival parking is a core feature, not a hidden optional screen. Request
 camera, nearby-device and precise-location permissions only when the relevant feature needs them.

 Bike while riding
 Use 800x480 as the tested ATR transport canvas, not a universal physical or unobstructed display
 size. Layout must use calibrated visible bounds and reserved areas. Prioritize turn arrow, distance to
 maneuver, street name and a simplified 2D map. ETA and distance are secondary. Arrival mode adds
 up to three stable parking pins with known prices attached directly to each pin; no parking footer or
 selection panel. Hide developer controls and diagnostics. Preserve any original vehicle indicators or
 warning areas that remain outside the projection viewport; do not imitate instrument readings or
 assume the display supports touch.

 Accessibility and legibility
 Phone controls should provide at least 48x48 dp touch targets; use larger practical targets where
 appropriate. Target WCAG AA text contrast, support font scaling and TalkBack on the phone, and
 never convey route status by color alone. Driver-oriented guidance favors short text and glanceable
 content; it is a design reference, not Android Auto certification of our custom protocol. Test daylight
 glare, darkness and vibration on the actual bike. [S30,S31]

 Fit my TFT: adaptive layouts and saved profiles
 Owner requirement: dashboards differ in aspect ratio, dimensions and native overlays. The ATR
 currently shows a footer during mirroring that can hide content and make a portrait phone screen
 appear cropped or distorted. Do not assume the full video canvas is visible or that the native footer
 can be removed.

 A navigation scene designed for the bike
 Render a dedicated navigation view independently from the phone UI. Negotiate supported video
 dimensions where the protocol permits, then reflow components into the calibrated safe viewport.
 Preserve geometry and font proportions; never non-uniformly stretch the UI to fill a different shape.
 Scale/letterbox when needed. Cropping may apply to map framing only, never hide turn instructions,
 prices, attribution or status text.

 Phone setup: Fit my TFT
 While parked, select a saved bike/profile or start a generic profile. Read reported transport
 capabilities and propose a canvas. Send a calibration screen with visible corner targets, grid, round
 circle and readable size samples. The phone shows a preview and draggable safe-area edges plus
 numeric/nudge controls for top, bottom, left and right. Reserve a footer or other overlay as an
 exclusion area. Adjust zoom/offset only within validated bounds, then save or reset. The bike view
 updates during calibration so the rider can compare it directly.

 Reported size versus visible size
 Show encoder canvas resolution separately from usable viewport and reserved insets. Manual
 visible-area calibration remains necessary if the firmware does not report native overlays. Do not
 label a phone preview as a live screenshot of the TFT unless we actually receive a display capture. A
 saved profile is a user override, not automatic evidence of firmware behavior.

 undistorted; all required labels fit. Simulate wide, compact and differently shaped viewports,
 overlapping exclusions, font scaling and portrait phone rotation. No parking label or maneuver may
 land under an overlay. If the usable area is too small, show a compact turn-first fallback and explain
 the limit in setup rather than silently clipping content.

 Reliability and battery are core features
 Architecture
 Keep five clear modules: bike connection, navigation engine, location/voice service, display
 renderer/encoder, and phone UI. Keep local routing and regional packages behind clear interfaces;
 optional cloud enhancements must not break offline navigation or violate data licenses. Model session
 state explicitly: connecting, connected, navigating, reconnecting, stopped. Own callbacks and sockets
 within a single session; make start/stop idempotent.

 Screen-off navigation is a development gate
 Render our navigation scene into a dedicated encoder surface or supported offscreen map pipeline.
 Avoid dependence on MediaProjection, a foreground phone Activity, screenshots of another app, or a
 per-frame network map-image request. The chosen map SDK must both permit and technically
 support this rendering path. Prototype it early; it is not yet proven by the screen-mirroring tests.

 Battery strategy
 Use one navigation location feed with suitable accuracy/update interval while moving and less activity
 while stationary. Keep interpolation and display animation separate from GPS frequency. Prefer
 simple 2D rendering and hardware H.264 at 800x480; evaluate roughly 10-15 fps rather than
 assuming more frames improve navigation. Cache only as licensed. Stop location, encoding and
 network work at trip end. Android documents frequent foreground navigation updates and reducing
 unnecessary location work. [S29]

 Parking before arrival: required MVP behavior
 The rider should see nearby motorcycle parking on the TFT before reaching the destination, including
 comparable prices where reliable data exists. This is a primary product requirement. It must work
 from the downloaded regional package when offline.

 Journey and trigger
 During route preview, select planned parking duration (for example two hours), maximum walking
 distance and preference: cheapest known price or shortest walk. Prepare candidates then. Proposed
 arrival trigger: remaining route distance at most 1 km OR route ETA at most 3 minutes. Tune with
 rides; use debounce and hysteresis so GPS noise does not repeatedly switch modes. Preload
 candidates earlier, not only when connectivity may already be lost.

 TFT information hierarchy
 Keep turn arrow, maneuver distance and next street visible. Show destination distinctly from parking.
 Display one to three non-overlapping P parking pins according to the active display profile. Attach a
 short known price and its basis directly to the pin, for example PHP40/2h. A lot with no reliable price
 keeps its parking icon with no price text, question mark or unknown badge. No parking footer,
 comparison strip, sidebar, selected-lot highlight, selection controls or automatic carousel. Parking is
 passive information on the TFT. Hide or reduce parking detail near complex/imminent maneuvers;
 retain the driving instruction.

                                     source and verification date.

   rate                              first-period wording if a full total cannot be calculated.

 Passive pins and the final meters
 Route to the motorcycle entrance, not a building centroid or inaccessible back wall. Account for
 one-way approach and public/customer/private access. If a parking destination was explicitly set on
 the phone before riding, retain the original destination for a walking leg after parking. Otherwise keep
 guiding to the original destination while displaying nearby pins. With no known suitable lots, show no
 parking pins; never invent one. Required attribution may remain in the map safe area, but not as a
 parking footer.

 Parking data: ownership, pricing and offline
 storage
 Accurate fees and entrances are the main data challenge. Start with 10-20 surveyed/permissioned
 Cebu lots around a few destinations rather than claiming complete city coverage. OSM includes
 parking, motorcycle parking, entrance, access, fee and charge tags, but optional tags do not
 guarantee complete or current local tariffs. [S44-S47]

 Regional record design
 Store stable lot ID, coordinates and entrance geometry; vehicle eligibility; access/public/customer
 restrictions; opening hours/time zone; tariff currency and structured rules; source and
 permission/license; verified-at and effective dates; freshness/confidence state; and any correction
 status. Keep live occupancy in a separate expiring layer. Retain raw source evidence privately only
 where permitted; do not publish reporter identities.

 Tariff calculation is deterministic
 Support first period plus subsequent increments, flat entry, daily caps, weekday/weekend and time
 bands, motorcycle-specific rates and explicit grace periods. Calculate the planned stay against the
 actual schedule and tariff version. If a reliable listed basis is usable, show that basis without inventing
 a total. Otherwise omit the TFT price label; retain the data-quality reason in phone details. A changed
 arrival time can change price; recalculate locally.

 Initial data workflow
 Use operator-published/permissioned information, direct verification and optional rider submissions.
 Photograph a rate board only with appropriate permission and privacy handling; record the original
 source, applicable vehicle and date. Moderation verifies and versions corrections. Do not scrape
 Google Maps/Places into our permanent parking database.

 Offline delivery and freshness
 Ship parking POIs and rates as a versioned SQLite layer alongside maps/search/graph, allowing small
 independent updates. Before departure, offer an update when available; retain the last good package
 if download fails. A proposed initial policy marks tariffs older than 30 days stale in phone details and
 suppresses their TFT amount until reverified; tune per operator reliability. The TFT retains the parking
 icon without a missing/stale-price badge. These thresholds are product defaults, not guarantees that
 a newer price is correct.

 Data quality tests
 Test motorcycles rejected, entrance across a one-way street, customer-only lots, overnight stays,
 midnight/weekend boundaries, missing fees, stale tariffs, conflicting reports, no occupancy feed and
 interrupted updates. Verify pin placement and price labels at calibrated ATR output and smaller/wider
 profile simulations, in sunlight, without covering guidance or the native footer. Rate tests must
 include fixture examples with known totals and boundary times.

 What should not use an LLM
 GPS tracking, graph search, route progress, safety restrictions, price arithmetic, tariff validity, pin
 placement and parking ranking should use ordinary tested code. Cheapest/nearest ranking is
 deterministic. AI cannot infer empty spaces, current fees or legal road access from the map.

 Cost policy
 Baseline app works with no AI account, no mandatory AI server and no paid inference calls. Prefer
 local OCR and templates. Cloud AI, live traffic and occupancy feeds are individually opt-in, with
 explicit budgets and a working off switch. A local LLM is a later experiment only if its memory, model
 size, latency and battery cost are justified.

 Interaction while riding
 The TFT shows the result, not an AI conversation. At most a brief spoken arrival summary,
 configurable before riding. Do not require riders to read long explanations, confirm a complex chat
 response or enter tariff data while moving.

 Download cost rather than trip cost
 Budget formula: download bandwidth = active devices x downloaded package size x number of
 full-equivalent downloads. For example, 100 devices downloading an illustrative 500 MB package
 once is about 50 GB; 1,000 devices is about 500 GB, before retries/updates. 500 MB is an example,
 not a measured Cebu package size. Measure actual map/graph/search size before committing to
 storage requirements or a hosting plan.
 Each additional ride on an already downloaded region need not make any map/navigation cloud
 request. Updates can be optional Wi-Fi downloads with clear package age. Separate small parking
 updates from larger map/graph releases. Prefer a budget-capped distribution service; free tiers are
 conveniences, not long-term guarantees.

 Planning allowance
 Keep a provisional $0-30/month small-beta hosting allowance only as an internal estimate; a quote
 needs measured package size, riders, downloads and bandwidth rates. Offline routing can require
 more implementation work than the previous commercial-SDK plan, so do not treat its earlier labor
 estimate as a fixed quote. Google Play registration is $25 once and applicable digital subscriptions
 may incur store fees. [S37,S38]

 Commercial pricing remains a fallback comparison
 Google Navigation bills destinations rather than map-display minutes; existing-destination reroutes
 are not separately charged under its documented SDK billing. Mapbox bills navigation sessions/users.
 Saving data locally does not automatically remove SDK charges or grant offline content rights.
 Neither SDK is in the offline baseline. [S48,S49]

 Can you market it in ATR 160 groups?
 Potentially, but this research cannot establish unconditional permission or QJMotor
 approval. An independent compatibility app and a misleading official product are different
 propositions. No ATR-160-specific public third-party projection approval/prohibition was located on the
 official Philippine site. That absence is not authorization. The actual owner manual, dealer warranty,
 dashboard firmware terms and accepted Carbit agreement still need review. [S34]

 Before public beta advertising
 Use your own name and artwork. Describe the tested model/phone/firmware accurately and avoid
 official/authorized/certified claims, QJMotor logos, copied Carbit artwork or unsupported warranty
 promises. Ask the specific group administrator about promotional rules; membership alone does not
 settle posting permission. Do not advertise all ATR 160 units as supported from a single successful
 test.

 Before charging or broad distribution
 Obtain a Philippine IP/software review of protocol research, code provenance, contractual restrictions
 and marketing language. Section 185 of the Philippine IP Code recognizes that interoperability
 decompilation may constitute fair use under its criteria; it is not a blanket commercial exemption or a
 substitute for analyzing contracts and other rights. Do not ship proprietary SDK binaries, extracted
 keys/assets or activation bypasses. Review GPL/AGPL obligations before incorporating reference code.
 [S35]
 For any optional commercial map data, clear terms for commercial navigation and projection. For the
 baseline, audit open-source component obligations, OSM attribution/ODbL and third-party data rights.
 The open-source decision does not establish QJMotor/Carbit approval. Google specifically restricts
 embedded vehicle use in general terms, while its SDK policies address permitted mobile navigation.
 Do not assume Android Auto examples authorize EasyConn/PXC. TomTom and HERE also need
 account-specific confirmation. [S6-S8]
 Treat identifiable location/history and diagnostic uploads as personal information. Document purpose,
 lawful basis, recipients, retention, security and deletion. Keep trip sharing and telemetry optional,
 minimize collection and publish a truthful privacy notice. The Philippine Data Privacy Act requires
 transparency, legitimate purpose and proportionality. [S36]
 Review business/tax registration, consumer terms, refunds, digital purchase rules and support
 obligations for the actual launch structure. Replace the prototype signing setup with a protected
 production key and finalized application identity; the shared test key is unsuitable for commercial
 release. Complete Play policies and testing, including the applicable new-personal-account closed test
 requirement. [S37-S39]

 Engineering preparation
 Migrate packaging to a standard Android Gradle project with version catalog, reproducible builds, CI
 compile/lint, protocol regression tests and recorded non-sensitive fixtures. Isolate display and
 navigation from Activity lifecycle. Keep credentials out of the repo, bind dashboard traffic to the
 correct interface, and leave Internet traffic on an available Internet network. Validate current Android
 foreground location/connected-device rules and store target API at release time.

 Product preparation
 Create the selected phone and bike design as editable production UI; specify day/night colors, type
 sizes, route symbols, state/error messages and attribution slots. Produce a clean standalone logo and
 app icon after selecting the concept. Generated maps, timings and routes are illustrations, not
 geographic assets or a functioning navigation build.

 What remains unresolved
 On-device engine integration and measured Cebu package size; 160 cc access correctness; screen-off
 rendering; long-ride reliability; verified parking rate/entrance coverage and maintenance; actual ATR
 warranty/software terms; name/trademark clearance. Optional traffic/occupancy/AI contracts are not
 baseline blockers. These are concrete development/launch gates, not reasons to keep expanding the
 diagnostic.

 Research method
 Official documentation, vendor pricing/terms, primary GitHub repositories/releases and Maven
 metadata were reviewed. Mobbin supplied a route-planning UX reference. Product Design and built-in
 image generation produced three visual/branding concepts. No competitor code or assets were
 imported into the app during this research. Websites and metadata can change; re-check terms,
 published artifacts and prices at integration and release.
