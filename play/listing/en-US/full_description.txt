Daanvia is offline motorcycle navigation for the Philippines. Download the area you ride once, and the map, search, turn-by-turn directions and parking keep working with no signal and no mobile data, in the mountains, in the province, or when your load runs out.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
• Download the part of the Philippines you ride, from Luzon to Mindanao. Daanvia shows the size in km and MB before you download.
• After that, the map, search, directions and parking work without mobile data.
• 1,650 towns are built in, so you can find yours before any download.

ROUTES FOR A 160 CC SCOOTER
• Routes are planned on your phone and set for a scooter, so they skip expressways and toll roads.
• Spoken directions before each turn, and a new route on its own if you miss one.
• Start at a gas station or a parking lot and the first direction tells you which way to turn onto the road.

SEARCH THE WAY YOU SAY IT
• Branches are named by road and town, like "Jollibee · EDSA, Makati".
• Type a place and its area, like "chowking guadalupe", and the right branch comes first.
• With signal, Daanvia also searches OpenStreetMap for new places and streets.

PARKING AND GATES
• See motorcycle parking near where you're going, nearest first, with hours and rates where known.
• Know before you go when the route passes guardhouse gates, or a place is inside a private subdivision.

DIRECTIONS ON YOUR BIKE'S DISPLAY
Some motorcycle dashboards connect to a phone by scanning a QR code on the dashboard (the EasyConn connection). Daanvia can show its own navigation screen on those dashboards over Wi-Fi Direct: the map, the next turn and the one after it, and the time and distance left.
• Calls and messages can show on the bike as a short card. You choose which apps, and nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
• Move the cards so your speedometer doesn't cover them.
• Mirror your phone's screen to the bike for music or another app.
You don't need such a dashboard: everything else in Daanvia works on the phone alone.

MADE FOR RIDING
• Rest the phone: a dark screen with just the next turn saves battery while the bike shows the directions.
• Day and night maps that switch at sunset where you are.
• Rides carry on if your phone closes the app mid-ride.
• Save places, or share them to Daanvia from Google Maps.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads and no tracking. Your location, routes and saved places stay on your phone. Read what Daanvia sends and why at daanvia-app.vercel.app/privacy.

Daanvia is a navigation aid. Plan your route before you ride, and always follow the road, signs and traffic rules. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Daanvia is not affiliated with any motorcycle maker or with Carbit.
